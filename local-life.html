Featured Highlight

Whale Watching in the Puget Sound

Living at Kala Point means being just a stone's throw away from some of the most spectacular marine life on the planet. The Puget Sound is a world-renowned destination for watching Humpbacks, Orcas, and Gray Whales in their natural habitat.

"There is nothing quite like the silence of the water being broken by the powerful breath of a whale. It reminds us of the incredible ecosystem we are privileged to call home."
